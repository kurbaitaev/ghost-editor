On every trip, I keep one paper thing from each day.
A ticket from the first train.
A receipt from a café I could never find again.
A leaf from a park, pressed between two pages.
My phone has three thousand photos from the same trips.
I never open them.
Storage almost full. Again.
But the box? I open it every winter.
Because a photo shows you where you were.
A ticket reminds you who you were there.
So on your next trip, keep the paper.
Your phone will forget. The box won't.
