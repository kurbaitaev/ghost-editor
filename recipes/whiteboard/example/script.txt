Your willpower works like a battery.
Every small choice drains it a little: what to wear, what to eat, what to watch tonight.
By evening it's almost empty, and that's when we make our worst decisions.
A tired brain takes the easy road: the snack, the scroll, the yes you regret.
So the fix isn't more discipline. It's fewer choices.
Decide the small things once: the same breakfast, the same clothes, the same time to train.
Then the one choice that really matters gets a full battery.
