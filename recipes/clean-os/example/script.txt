# Clean OS worked example: "one thing at a time" (9 lines, ~95 words, about 32 s).
# One talking-head take per line, read calmly to camera. cut.json holds the source seconds of the best complete take of
# each line in a stand-in recording; re-time them from your own transcript.
You don't need more time. You need fewer tabs.
This morning I counted 32 open tabs, 11 unread chats and 3 half-written drafts.
Every one of them is a small promise to come back later.
And every promise takes a little piece of your attention.
So try this. Pick one thing and close everything else.
Set a timer for 25 minutes and turn your notifications off.
Not forever. Just until the timer rings.
Then you'll have one thing finished, instead of ten things started.
And that feeling is better than any app.
