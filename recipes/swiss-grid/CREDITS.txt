WHAT WAS ADAPTED (MIT, with the notice above)
  - Spec text: the style's rules, paraphrased and reworked for a 9:16 talking head in SYSTEM.md and house.json
    (modular grid, one typeface, one signal colour, flush left / ragged right, extreme scale contrast, subtitles as
    part of the layout, clip-reveal words on their onsets, two curves only, wipes not fades, knife-cut deletions,
    ripples ordered by distance, the exception that moves linearly and unsnapped with an on-grid / off-grid readout,
    giant numerals climbing in four eased steps and set optically flush left, caption rows with a rule above,
    print foley, the "grid means something" and "the exception leaves the frame" moves).
  - Component code: the precise ease cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1) solver (demo/ease.js cubicBezier -> SW in swissgrid.py's
    page script), the note-value timing (snap lands one note value after it starts), the stepped numeral climb
    (ease.js steps), the clip-rect word reveal and upward exit (film.js revealText / drawLine), the knife cut (film.js
    drawPosterText knife line + midline collapse), the distance-ordered ripple (film.js buildUnits rank), the
    exception's linear flight with a pause (film.js flight). Re-written from canvas into HTML + GSAP for HyperFrames.
  - Palette: page #EAE9E5, paper #FFFFFF, ink #111111, signal red #E30613, grey guides #CFCEC9 family (DEMO.md
    "Palette & props").
