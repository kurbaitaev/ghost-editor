What was adapted (rewritten for HyperFrames + a vertical talking head, not copied file for file)
  - Spec text: the style's essence, layout zones, editing grammar, caption card, keyword block, frame-4 hook, title
    sting -> bug, source tag, document-with-highlighter, lit grid, ledger sheet, sound-per-action rules (STYLE.md
    sections 1-8), paraphrased into SYSTEM.md and house.json.
  - Component code: capCard() (chunk slam scale 1.3 -> 1 in 0.13 s, back 1.6, keyword wipe 0.12 s), hook(), bug(),
    sourceTag(), shotSting() (ruled honey page, red double margin, title slam, page wipe-off), paper() / shotDoc6()
    (paper on a dark desk, tilt, push, multiply highlighter from bbox coordinates, re-set block), shotGrid() (staggered
    panels lit on their words), ledger() (ruled sheet, red margin, slide-in with speed streaks), num() (fixed-width
    digits): their timings and geometry, re-implemented in captionnews.py.
  - Palette: ink #0E0E0C, ivory #F7F5EF, honey #FFC21A, loss red #FF4A3D, grey #8D8B83, #5E5C55, ledger #151512,
    desk #23221E, and their meanings.
