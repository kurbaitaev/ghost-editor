ADAPTED (MIT)

    numbers (target lock 1.9x / 45 deg / 0.22 s / 3-frame flash, rolling values right to left, chip dock, explode along
    the assembly axis, one 3-frame glitch), HUD furniture and the native moves, rewritten for a vertical talking head in
    SYSTEM.md and house.json.
  - Component code: engine/holohud.js is adapted from demo/engine/holo.js (projection, normalisation, the wireframe
    renderer with depth fog, scan and scan-erase, solid Fresnel faces, glow, pad, scan plane) and demo/engine/hud.js
    (theme, background, plate, chrome, target box, leader, rollText, monoText, spec card, chip, marker, beam, subtitle,
    glitch). Changes: classic script, 1080x1920, Cyrillic fonts and glyphs, 48 px floor, toggle chips, multi-part locks,
    new models, and a data-driven scene renderer.
